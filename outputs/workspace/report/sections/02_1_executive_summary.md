## 1. Executive Summary

Lumivaara Logistics Oy is a Finnish family-owned third-party logistics company with an AI maturity level preliminarily assessed by FAIR as Low: its digital operations are established, but AI use is individual and there is no AI strategy, policy or roadmap (company_overview_2026.pdf, page 2; advisory_meeting_notes.txt, “2. CURRENT USE OF AI AND DIGITAL TOOLS”). Its top-priority use case is freight document processing, where AI would extract document fields into the TMS, reducing manual data entry and related errors, while flagging handwritten damage remarks to support timely claims; uncertain results would go to staff for review (advisory_meeting_notes.txt, “3.1 Freight document processing (highest priority for the company)”; advisory_meeting_recording_part2.mp3). The main recommendation is to begin with a proof of concept for proof-of-delivery documents at the Vantaa site, testing on real documents, including poor-quality images and handwriting, and comparing extraction accuracy with clerk entries (advisory_meeting_recording_part3.mp3; process_map_freight_documents.png).
