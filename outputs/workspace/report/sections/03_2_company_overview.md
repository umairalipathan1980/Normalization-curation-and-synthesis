## 2. Company Overview

Lumivaara Logistics Oy is a Finnish family-owned third-party logistics company founded by the Lindholm family in 2009. Kaisa Lindholm took over from her parents and became CEO in 2021. (advisory_meeting_recording_part1.mp3; advisory_meeting_notes.txt, 1. COMPANY BACKGROUND; company_overview_2026.pdf, page 2; advisory_meeting_notes.txt, 1. COMPANY BACKGROUND) The company is headquartered in Vantaa and operates three warehouses in Vantaa, Tampere and Oulu. Its services include contract warehousing, domestic road freight, e-commerce fulfilment, customs clearance, and value-added services such as labelling, kitting and returns handling. (company_overview_2026.pdf, page 2; advisory_meeting_notes.txt, 1. COMPANY BACKGROUND)

Unresolved: The meeting recording and notes estimate around 130 employees, with temporary agency staff in peak seasons in addition, while the company overview reports 134 employees measured as full-time equivalents at the end of 2025, also complemented by temporary agency staff during peak seasons. (advisory_meeting_recording_part1.mp3; advisory_meeting_notes.txt, 1. COMPANY BACKGROUND; company_overview_2026.pdf, page 2) Revenue in 2025 was EUR 24.6 million. (company_overview_2026.pdf, page 2) Lumivaara serves about 210 active business customers. Its largest customer segments are retail and e-commerce, industrial spare parts, chilled food and beverage, and pharma and healthcare; the pharma and healthcare segment was the fastest-growing and highest-margin segment in 2025. (advisory_meeting_recording_part1.mp3; company_overview_2026.pdf, page 5) The company faces narrowing margins, rising wages and fuel costs, and price pressure from large retail customers. Several large retail contracts are due for renewal in 2027. (advisory_meeting_recording_part1.mp3; company_overview_2026.pdf, page 5) Its strategy aims to reduce manual office work by 30% by the end of 2028, supporting growth without adding office staff for each new customer. (company_overview_2026.pdf, page 11; advisory_meeting_recording_part1.mp3)
