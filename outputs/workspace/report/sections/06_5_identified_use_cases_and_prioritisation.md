The proposed initial scope is one document type, proof of delivery, at the Vantaa site, with expansion if the pilot works (advisory_meeting_recording_part3.mp3).

Unresolved: which use case comes next. The meeting recording presents forecasting as second after freight document processing and customer service as third.
