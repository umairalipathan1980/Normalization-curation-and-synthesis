## 8. Identified Target Market and Customer Segments

The company serves about 210 active business customers. Retail and e-commerce is its largest segment, followed by industrial spare parts, chilled food and beverage, and pharma and healthcare. In 2025, these segments accounted for 41%, 27%, 18% and 9% of revenue, respectively; other customers accounted for 5%. Pharma and healthcare was the fastest-growing segment, with 31% growth in 2025. (company_overview_2026.pdf, page 5; advisory_meeting_recording_part1.mp3)

Retail customers apply the strongest price pressure, and several large retail contracts are due for renewal in 2027. Only about one third of key customers regularly provide volume forecasts.
