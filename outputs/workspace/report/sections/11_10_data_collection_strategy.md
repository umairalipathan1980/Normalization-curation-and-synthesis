## 10. Data Collection Strategy

The company has no central data warehouse, and reporting relies on manual Excel exports prepared by the IT Manager. Customer master data is maintained separately in the WMS, TMS and Business Central, with no shared customer identifier between the systems (company_overview_2026.pdf, page 8). The WMS is an on-premise SQL Server system, and the IT Manager has read-only SQL access. Its current data format dates from the 2020 upgrade; older data is not directly comparable because the database structure changed (operations_data_extract.xlsx, sheet Data_Inventory, row 2; advisory_meeting_recording_part2.mp3).

Data ownership is unresolved. The meeting notes state that no data owner roles are defined (advisory_meeting_notes.txt, 4. DATA AND SYSTEMS). By contrast, the data inventory assigns the Operations Director ownership of WMS data, TMS shipment data and the TMS document log, and assigns other datasets to roles such as the Customer Service Team Lead and HR manager (operations_data_extract.xlsx, sheet Data_Inventory, row 2). **Unresolved:** the sources differ on whether data owner roles are defined.

TMS access is through a REST API for individual records; bulk export is a paid service. For paper documents, the TMS log records the scan date as the registration date. The scanned freight-document archive consists of image-only PDFs without a text layer, has no index, and uses inconsistent file names (operations_data_extract.xlsx, sheet Data_Inventory, rows 3–5). Weekly customer volume forecasts have been available since 2022 from about 12 customers, but arrive irregularly in different Excel formats and are often missing (operations_data_extract.xlsx, sheet Data_Inventory, row 10). The shared customer-service mailbox contains consumer personal data, and categories are not stored in the email dataset (operations_data_extract.xlsx, sheet Data_Inventory, row 8).

**Unresolved:** one estimate puts freight-document volume at around 1,200 per day across all sites; another puts it at around 1,000 on a normal working day according to the TMS log, which counts paper documents when they are scanned (advisory_meeting_recording_part1.mp3; advisory_meeting_recording_part2.mp3). The company agreed to provide about 200 anonymized freight documents by the end of October 2026, covering different types, sites and languages, including at least 50 with handwritten remarks, and, if possible, the clerks’ corresponding entries (advisory_meeting_recording_part3.mp3). A planned extract by 30 October 2026 includes daily WMS volumes per site since 2020 and the TMS document log for 2025 (advisory_meeting_notes.txt, 8. ACTION ITEMS).

As of September 2026, the company had no written policy on generative AI use, while several customer contracts restrict transfers of customer data outside the EU/EEA (company_overview_2026.pdf, page 11).
