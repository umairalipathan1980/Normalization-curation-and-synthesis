## 12. Risks, Compliance and Responsible AI

Employees use the free version of ChatGPT mainly for translations and sometimes for customer letters. In spring 2026, the company also piloted Copilot licences for 25 office staff. Usage was uneven, and training consisted of one vendor webinar. (advisory_meeting_recording_part1.mp3) The CEO said they did not know whether customer data was being entered into public AI tools, while the company overview identifies uncontrolled use of public AI tools with customer data as a risk. Unresolved: the sources do not establish whether this use is confirmed or remains uncertain. (advisory_meeting_recording_part1.mp3; company_overview_2026.pdf, page 12) The company has no AI-use policy, strategy or roadmap, and as of September 2026 had no written policy for generative AI tools. (advisory_meeting_recording_part1.mp3; company_overview_2026.pdf, page 11)

Customer service emails may contain consumers’ names, home addresses and phone numbers. The advisory discussion raised questions about GDPR, where this data is processed, and transparency obligations under the AI Act. (advisory_meeting_recording_part3.mp3) Contract restrictions on data transfers also need to be considered: the meeting discussion referred to some pharma contracts restricting transfers outside the EU, while the company overview says several customer contracts, especially in pharma, restrict transfers outside the EU/EEA. Unresolved: the sources give different geographic boundaries. (advisory_meeting_recording_part3.mp3; company_overview_2026.pdf, page 11) The IT manager said AI services would need to run in the EU; the advisor noted that major cloud providers offer EU data residency. (advisory_meeting_recording_part3.mp3)

The company has a privacy statement and record of processing activities, and the CFO is the data protection contact; no data protection officer has been appointed. Information security is managed with an outsourcing partner and includes Microsoft 365 multi-factor authentication, endpoint protection and backups of the on-premise WMS. (company_overview_2026.pdf, page 11) Data-owner roles have not been defined, and customer master data is duplicated between the TMS and Business Central without a common customer ID. (advisory_meeting_notes.txt, 4. DATA AND SYSTEMS) The company has one IT person, identified as a potential bottleneck, so the proposed solution needs to be low-maintenance or operated by a partner. (advisory_meeting_notes.txt, 7. ADVISORS' OBSERVATIONS (internal, Elina & Daniel); advisory_meeting_recording_part3.mp3) For document extraction, handwriting was identified as the main risk, and testing on real documents was considered necessary. (advisory_meeting_recording_part2.mp3) The proposed customer-service system would require an agent to approve every AI-drafted reply, at least initially; automatic customs declarations were considered too risky to pursue for now.
