## 14. Expectations from FAIR Services

The company wanted FAIR’s help to choose and scope its first proof of concept, with freight document processing as its leading candidate. It also sought technical advice on document extraction options, TMS API integration and human-in-the-loop design, as well as an assessment of whether WMS data was suitable for forecasting. (advisory_meeting_notes.txt, 6. EXPECTATIONS FROM FAIR)

The company requested guidance on an AI usage policy and on GDPR and AI Act obligations, particularly for customer service. The advisors recommended a usage policy and short practical Copilot training, and offered to send a policy template. (advisory_meeting_notes.txt, 6. EXPECTATIONS FROM FAIR; advisory_meeting_recording_part3.mp3)

The company was interested in having a Master's thesis student work on forecasting in spring 2027. It also wanted help with a funding application if the proof of concept looked promising, and expressed interest in public funding from Business Finland or the ELI Center. The company said it had not applied for such funding before and would need help; the advisors offered assistance with parts of the application and a list of possible funding instruments. (advisory_meeting_notes.txt, 6. EXPECTATIONS FROM FAIR; advisory_meeting_recording_part2.mp3; advisory_meeting_recording_part3.mp3)
